# Risks

## Opportunity fragmentation
Many plausible opportunities can compete for attention.

## Technology-driven overbuilding
Technical ability can make sophisticated implementation attractive before validation.

## Research replacing execution
Frameworks and market analysis are valuable until they delay customer contact or revenue experiments.

## Content intensity
Sustained daily publishing has previously created burnout concerns.

## Novelty attraction
Emerging AI technology can pull attention from commercially important but boring work.

These are behavioral hypotheses, not fixed personality diagnoses.
